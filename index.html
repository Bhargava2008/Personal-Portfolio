I am Bhargav. A developer, designer, creator and an aspiring entrepreneur who enjoys building things from scratch. Bhargav has always been curious about how things work. Bhargav finds excitement in taking an idea that exists in Bhargavs mind and turning it into something people can actually use. Bhargav is currently studying technology while constantly learning, experimenting and working on projects that push Bhargav beyond what Bhargav knows. Bhargav enjoys being involved in parts of the process. From designing an interface and writing code to thinking about the product, the user and the bigger idea behind it. Bhargav has worked with technologies such as HTML, CSS, JavaScript, Java, Node.js, Express.js, MongoDB, SQL, React, Git and Linux and Bhargav is continuously expanding skills. Alongside technology Bhargav is interested in startups, business, design, marketing and product building. Bhargav is the founder of Minimal Pixels, an agency and startup focused on connecting creators and businesses with quick accessible creative services such as thumbnails, video editing, social media designs, branding and more. Building Minimal Pixels has taught Bhargav that creating a product is not about writing code. It is, about understanding people solving real problems testing ideas and being willing to start before everything is perfect. Bhargav is still learning, still experimenting and still figuring things out. And honestly that is what Bhargav enjoys most. Bhargav does not just want to learn technology; Bhargav wants to use technology to build things that matter.
